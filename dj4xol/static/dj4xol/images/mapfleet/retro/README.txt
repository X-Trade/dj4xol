Retro fleet sprite source
=========================

These spaceship sprites were derived from the "16x16 Ship Collection"
asset pack on OpenGameArt:

https://opengameart.org/content/1616-ship-collection

Original author: Master484
Website: http://m484games.ucoz.com/

Author attribution note from the source page:
"Attribution is not needed. But if you wish to give me credit, then you can
mention 'Master484' as the original author, and maybe also put a link to my
website: http://m484games.ucoz.com/"

The sprites in this folder were extracted, trimmed, palette-matched, and
reorganized for dj4xol Retro Arcade fleet map icons.
