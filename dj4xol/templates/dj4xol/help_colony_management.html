Colony Management Basics

Simple Early Rule

On young colonies, a safe default priority is usually mines, then factories, then labs.

Mines First

Colonies need minerals to grow. Mines pull resources from underground deposits, and without minerals your colony quickly stalls out on later construction.

Factories Next

Factories create BP (Build Points). Most production orders depend on BP, so factories are what let a colony accelerate beyond its initial setup.

Labs After That

Labs matter, but very early colonies often need to become self-supporting before they can spare population and minerals for research infrastructure. For more detail, see Research & Labs.

Shipyards

Keep at least one shipyard on an important colony. Shipyards are required to build fleets, repair damaged ones, and refuel fleets in orbit.

Staffing Still Matters

Infrastructure also needs colonists to work it. If you move too much population away, output and growth can both suffer.

Use the Calculator

If you want to inspect productivity and staffing more closely, open the Colony Calculator.
