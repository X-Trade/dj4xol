Colony Management Basics

Simple Early Rule

On young colonies, a safe default priority is usually mines, then factories, then labs.

Mines First

Colonies need minerals to grow. Mines pull resources from underground deposits, and without minerals your colony quickly stalls out on later construction.

Factories Next

Factories create BP (Build Points). Most production orders depend on BP, so factories are what let a colony accelerate beyond its initial setup.

Labs After That

Labs matter, but very early colonies often need to become self-supporting before they can spare population and minerals for research infrastructure. For more detail, see Research & Labs.

Shipyards

Keep at least one shipyard on an important colony. Shipyards are required to build fleets, repair damaged ones, and refuel fleets in orbit.

Colony AI Administration

Administration automates production planning for a colony. If a colony has no Administration built, no automation runs there.

Administration has four tiers. Higher tiers keep lower-tier behavior and add more:

  • Tier 1 (Basic): Manages mines, factories, and defenses. It can also add up to one lab and one shipyard when the colony is already in a surplus position.
  • Tier 2 (Support): Also manages labs and shipyards, can take over your unstarted repeating infrastructure orders, and aims for a support mix where factories stay roughly double labs and defenses.
  • Tier 3 (Terraform): Adds terraforming order planning. If Dyson Sphere tech is unlocked, it can also start planning toward one.
  • Tier 4 (Logistics): Keeps Tier 3 production behavior and also dispatches idle fleets for resource logistics between colonies and asteroid fields.

Planning Horizon By Tier

Tier 2 can queue shipyards using a longer plan (up to about 5 years ahead). Tier 3+ can plan even longer for Dyson Sphere work (up to about 9 years ahead).

Jobs Guardrails

Administration plans around jobs-to-population targets: roughly 25% minimum, 50% comfort target, and 75% upper bound. The 75% cap is strict for adding shipyards and Dyson Spheres. Mines and factories can be treated more flexibly during bootstrap and catch-up phases.

Tier 4 Logistics Limits

Automatic logistics keeps a local defense reserve before dispatching fleets, pulls missing resources from your colonies first, then asteroid fields, and can optionally redistribute excess. It is limited to one automatic dispatch per colony per year.

Staffing Still Matters

Infrastructure also needs colonists to work it. If you move too much population away, output and growth can both suffer.
