How to Colonise

Colonising uses up the fleet. The colonise order scuttles it onto the target world and leaves all carried cargo behind.

Basic Sequence

  1. Pick a fleet at your homeworld.
  2. Use Transfer to load colonists.
  3. Do not drain the homeworld completely. Leave enough population behind to keep the colony productive.
  4. Load useful starting minerals too, especially ironium.
  5. Give the fleet a Move to order for the target star.
  6. Add a Colonise order for that same destination.

What to Bring

Colonists are mandatory. Minerals are optional, but early colonies benefit a lot from starting ironium because mines and other infrastructure consume it quickly.

Common Mistake

New players often load too many colonists from the homeworld. Avoid crippling your first colony just to found the second. A smaller but sustainable transfer is usually better than emptying the capital.

When the Fleet Arrives

If the star is still unowned and the fleet has colonists aboard, the colonise order will found the colony, transfer the fleet cargo onto the surface, and remove the fleet.

After Landing

Your new colony normally wants mines first so it can feed its own construction. See Colony Management Basics for the next step.
