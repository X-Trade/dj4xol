Diplomacy

The Diplomacy screen lets you choose a standing toward every race you have encountered, plus a default standing for unknown races.

Standings now affect combat behavior, colony defensive posture, visiting shipyard service levels, and allied intel/scanner sharing.

When Changes Apply

Changes take effect on the next generated turn, rather than immediately.

When another race changes their stance toward you and it is applied, you receive a diplomatic message. The race name in that message links directly to their row in the Diplomacy screen.

First Contact & Defaults

Your default standing is used for races you have not dealt with before. Once a race has its own row, you can manage that relationship separately.

Each empire keeps its own standings. Your setting toward another race can differ from their setting toward you.

Effect on Combat Initiation

When fleets from two races meet, the game compares both standings and rolls for combat. Hostile against another hostile or cold stance always turns violent, and hostile contacts against more trusting stances are still very likely to do so.

Some race traits can bias the final outcome away from the table value, making hostile encounters slightly more or less likely than the baseline shown here.

Readiness Advantage

If combat starts between mismatched standings, the more hostile side gains a readiness advantage and the less hostile side suffers a matching disadvantage. This represents one side approaching contact more aggressively while the other is less prepared for violence.

In practical terms, a strongly hostile empire is more likely to strike first and perform better in opening contact than a race that expected a neutral or friendly meeting.

Current Limits

Current diplomacy effects include encounter combat initiation, readiness advantage in mismatched contacts, colony defense behavior, allied repair/refuel support, transfer hostility behavior, and allied intel/scanner sharing. Trade treaties, movement-right treaties, and granular per-permission controls are not yet available.
