Diplomacy

The Diplomacy screen lets you choose a standing toward every race you have encountered, plus a default standing for unknown races. Your default is copied into a new diplomacy entry the first time you identify another race.

Standings are currently used only for combat initiation. Later updates can attach additional effects to warmer or more hostile relations.

First Contact & Defaults

When you make first contact with a race, the game creates a specific diplomacy row for that race using your current default standing. After that, changing the default does not retroactively change existing race-specific entries.

Each empire keeps its own standings. Your setting toward another race can differ from their setting toward you.

Effect on Combat Initiation

When fleets from two races meet, the game compares both standings and rolls for combat. Any encounter involving Hostile is guaranteed to escalate into combat.

Readiness Advantage

If combat starts between mismatched standings, the more hostile side gains a readiness advantage and the less hostile side suffers a matching disadvantage. This represents one side approaching contact more aggressively while the other is less prepared for violence.

In practical terms, a strongly hostile empire is more likely to strike first and perform better in opening contact than a race that expected a neutral or friendly meeting.

Current Limits

Diplomacy currently affects whether a fleet encounter turns into combat and gives the more hostile side an edge when it does. It does not yet change trade, scanner sharing, movement rights, or other strategic systems.
