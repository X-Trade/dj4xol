Anomalies

What Are Anomalies?

Anomalies are non-standard map objects that can appear when a game is created with anomalies enabled. They can also spawn very rarely during play.

Types

Current anomaly types include Nebula, Comet, and Rift. They are visual map markers that fleets can target like other map objects.

Interaction Rule

If a fleet ends a year on an anomaly location, an anomaly interaction roll is applied. Outcomes include no effect, integrity damage, cargo disruption, fleet loss, or research gains.

Research Outcomes

Positive anomaly outcomes can grant bonus RP or major category progress immediately. These apply in addition to normal lab output.

Visibility & Reports

Anomalies can be selected on the starmap and are recorded in standard exploration reports when observed.
