Anomalies

What Are Anomalies?

Anomalies are non-standard map objects that can appear when a game is created with anomalies enabled. They can also spawn very rarely during play.

Types

Current anomaly types include Nebula, Comet, and Rift. They are visual map markers that fleets can target like other map objects.

Interaction Rule

If a fleet ends a year on an anomaly location, an anomaly interaction roll is applied. Outcomes include no effect, integrity damage, cargo disruption, fleet loss, or research gains.

Danger & Stability

Every anomaly has a Danger rating and a Stability rating. Danger decides what kinds of outcomes are on the table. Stability decides how strong the damage or reward is when something does happen.

In plain terms: higher danger means riskier and more lucrative possibilities, while lower stability makes the results hit harder. Even dangerous anomalies can still do nothing on a given visit.

Outcome Pattern

Each visit makes one roll on a simple six-slot outcome row. Lower danger rows lean toward harmless results, while high danger keeps the best rewards and the worst failures in play.

Danger Outcome Row
Low No Effect, No Effect, No Effect, Damage, Cargo Loss, Low RP
Medium No Effect, No Effect, Damage, Cargo Loss, Low RP, Medium RP
High No Effect, Damage, Cargo Loss, Destruction, Medium RP, Breakthrough

Cargo loss only matters if the fleet is carrying something. Otherwise that bad result effectively behaves like extra damage pressure instead.

Research Outcomes

Positive anomaly outcomes can grant bonus RP or a major breakthrough immediately. These apply in addition to normal lab output.

A breakthrough is rare, but it can complete an entire research category at once. Lower-tier rewards are more common and add RP to a random category.

Practical Advice

Low danger anomalies are usually safe to sample with ordinary fleets. High danger anomalies are best treated as gambles: they still sometimes do nothing, but they are the only ones that can destroy a fleet and the only ones that can produce a full breakthrough.

Visibility & Reports

Anomalies can be selected on the starmap and are recorded in standard exploration reports when observed.
