Open Source 4X, Built for Slow Strategy

Build an empire over weeks, not minutes.

DJ4XOL is a free and open source turn-based 4X space strategy game inspired by classic 90s PBEM design. You expand from a homeworld, manage colonies and minerals, commission fleets, research technologies, and resolve each turn through deliberate planning.

This is the opposite of fast-paced. Join an hourly or daily generation game and it becomes a passive 10-minute check-in habit.

How It Plays

  • Choose or create a game, then design your race and starting approach.
  • Plan production and fleet orders between turn generations.
  • Resolve movement, combat, invasions, and economy on each turn.
  • Read reports and messages, then queue your next decisions.
