Research & Labs

Lab Output

Research points are generated by labs at each owned colony. Lab production is affected by colony staffing and productivity.

Additional RP Sources

If enabled by race settings, unused build points can convert to RP. Leftover race creation points can also provide a one-time RP bonus on the first turn.

Budget Allocation

Generated RP is distributed across research categories according to your budget allocations. With singular research enabled, all RP is focused into one category at a time.

Unlock Rules

A technology is unlocked when your level in that technology's research category reaches or exceeds the technology's required level.

Practical Planning

To accelerate a specific capability, concentrate allocation into the corresponding category and keep colonies well staffed so labs remain productive.
