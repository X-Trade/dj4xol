Unknown Substances

What They Are (Or Aren't)

Certain materials do not appear in standard inventories or routine transfer dialogs. They surface only when something truly unusual has been found, handled, or brought home.

Discovery

You will not see them listed until you have encountered them directly. Reports, scanners, and the research ledger may hint that something exists before it has a name.

Usage

These substances can unlock late-stage research and may also be required in advanced production. Expect them to influence strategic priorities once you know what you are looking at.

Anomalous Echoes

Encounters with rare phenomena have been known to interact with exotic cargo. Some explorers report that bringing unusual material into an anomaly produces unexpected results.
