Exploration & First Turns

You usually begin with one or two basic fleets. Your first job is to find new worlds worth colonising.

Typical Opening

  1. Send your starting fleets out with the Move to order.
  2. Spread them across nearby stars instead of duplicating routes.
  3. Inspect your homeworld while they travel so production does not stall.

With Scanners

Fleets with scanners can reveal basic habitability of stars in range. On the starmap this appears as a brown circular underlay when the scanner overlay is toggled on.

Tap the scanner button to compare the same map with the overlay on and off.

In this example, A is inside advanced range, so you can expect the best ranged report. B is outside advanced range but still inside basic range, so you only get the lighter basic report. C is out of range, so you need to move closer or visit it directly.

Players just starting a new game often will not have the green advanced zone yet. Early scanner fleets usually only show the wider brown basic range until better scanner technology is unlocked.

Without Scanners

Fleets without scanners must visit each star directly before you get useful information. This makes route planning and coverage much more important in the opening turns.

Checking a Star

Clicking a star opens the detail panel. That report tells you whether the world is habitable and how much population it can support. Use that before committing colonists.

While Your Scouts Are Away

Check your homeworld production. A simple early priority is usually mines, then factories, then labs. Keep at least one shipyard so the colony can build, repair, and refuel fleets.

Once you find a good world, continue with the How to Colonise page.
